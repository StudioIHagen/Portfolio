JOHN BLOND HOUSE

Workshop in Favara, Sicily, with colletivo ctrl+S, Farm Cultural Park and Politecnico di Milano. We were given the task of transforming a house painted by the artist John Blond. Our proposal celebrates the artists art by preserving and playing with it in creative ways.
